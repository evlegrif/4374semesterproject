1. SpotHero
Focuses on off-street parking and partners with commercial garages and lots to offer discounted reserved spots
Strengths:
    Great UIUX
    Strong presence in major metropolitan areas and near airports
    States prices upfront and often has QR codes at the entry of lots to reduce friction
Weaknesses:
    Limited presence in on-street parking
    Relies on garages updating their availability, potentially leading to a double booking or time out if the API cannot resolve it fast enough
    High commission fees

2. ParkMobile
Main medium for users to search for on-street and municipal parking spaces. Users park before paying
Strengths:
    Large user count due to exclusive contracts with city transportation departments
    Zone-based model is easy to use for users
    Users can extend duration through the app
Weaknesses: 
    Lacks advanced reservations
    Convenience fees
    No navigation to spots due to structure of app

3. ParkWhiz
Similar to SpotHero but with greater focus on B2B API integrations and event parking
Strengths:
    Highly reliable API network that supports many significant event venues/event ticketing companies
    Good UIUX for operators
Weaknesses:
    Less market share than SpotHero
    Venue level errors are passed onto ParkWhiz
