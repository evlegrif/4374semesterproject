**Introduction**
Software Corp is a development studio with unlimited resources and budget. We are based in Sugar Land, TX.
***Project Acquisition**
Software Corp acquired this proeject after a rigorous bid competition for a Request for Proposal (RFP) that was issued by a collective of various Texas metropolitan transportation departments. This coalition of departments requested a single tool to reduce their drivers' time spent searching for available parking to reduce congestion within their cities. Additionally, they needed the tool to be easy to use on the operational side, with the capabilities to monitor occupancy, manage pricing, and improve utilization of parking assets. 
**Project Overview**
Software Corp plans to deliver a platform for real-time availability and reserving for drivers. Additionally, there will be an interactive map with directions. We will implement precise capacities and allow for seamless operator updating, administrative dashboards, and reporting and analytics. 

Software Corp will assign one team member to provide weekly updates for stakeholders, starting 9/10/26.
