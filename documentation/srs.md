**Drivers**
1. Register and authenticate account
2. Add vehicle license plate data
3. Open interactive map to view parking spots live
4. Reserve an advanced parking spot
5. Process payments digitally
6. Launches external default navigation
7. Extend parking session from app
8. Cancel reservation
9. Retrieve receipts and view reservation history
10. Receives push notifications (reservation ending soon, ended, etc.)
**Operators**
11. Administrative dashboard
12. Occupancy reporting and analytics
13. Map of parking spots showing live capacities
14. Pricing can be adjusted on the fly
**System**
15. Drivers can enter facilities through license plate readers
16. System sends push notifications 
17. All fees considered in price presented to user
18. Venue and ticketing companies remain responsible for third party bookings
